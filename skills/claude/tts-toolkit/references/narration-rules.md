# Narration Rules

Use these rules when preparing Markdown, TXT, HTML, EPUB, or PDF for TTS narration.

## Include

- Body paragraphs in full
- Important callouts, sidebars, examples, and highlighted content
- Spoken section headings at major boundaries

## Exclude Or Replace

- References, bibliographies, attributions, image credits, and licensing text
- Figure captions and decorative image markers
- Superscripts, endnotes, and footnote markers
- Raw table data; replace with: "The source includes a table here. Refer to the original text for the full details."

## TTS Formatting

- Keep sentences reasonably short when revising awkward prose
- Spell out abbreviations when they would sound odd
- Avoid all-caps text
- Keep long parentheticals from interrupting the main sentence
- Insert `[SECTION]` on its own line between major sections

Preserve the author's meaning and body text. The goal is listenable narration,
not a summary.
